Gmail App Password Setup

Google accounts with 2-Step Verification require a dedicated 16-character App Password to connect via SMTP.

  1. Open your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/security.
  2. Verify that 2-Step Verification is enabled.
  3. Search for or open App passwords.
  4. Create a password named PrivilegeGuard AI.
  5. Paste the 16-character password into your local .env file:
    SMTP_PASSWORD=xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx

After updating .env, click "Send Test Email" to verify live connectivity.
